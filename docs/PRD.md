# Product Requirements Document (`docs/PRD.md`)

> Maintained automatically across every stage. Keep Section 1 crystal clear in plain English so non-developers and developers stay 100% aligned.

## 1. Plain-English Overview
- **Product Name:** **`NairaBooks`** *(working workspace directory: `ac`)*
- **One-Sentence Pitch:** A Nigerian-first, IFRS-compliant accounting and business operations platform (built to surpass QuickBooks, ERPNext, Sage, and TallyPrime) that dynamically tailors its chart of accounts, tax rules, operational workflows, screens, and KPIs to fit a very wide range of Nigerian businesses—shipping with **6 Flagship Reference Packs** (*Retail & E-commerce*, *Wholesale & Distribution*, *Manufacturing (Light/Small Scale)*, *Construction & Trades*, *Professional Services*, *Non-Profit Organizations*) plus a **Universal Custom/Composable Pack Builder** (powering *Hotels/Hospitality*, *Diners/Restaurants*, *Healthcare*, *Logistics*, and hybrid multi-unit businesses).
- **Who Uses It:** Nigerian business owners, founders, finance controllers, ICAN/ANAN accountants, NGO treasurers, and frontline staff (POS cashiers, warehouse clerks, production supervisors, site engineers, project consultants, hotel/restaurant operators).
- **Problem Solved:** Foreign tools (QuickBooks, Sage, TallyPrime, ERPNext) bill in unstable FX or require expensive consulting, lack native Nigerian statutory reporting (FRCN IFRS / IFRS for SMEs + Statement of Value Added + 5-Year Summary, Nigeria Tax Act 2025 small-company rules, 2024/2025 WHT brackets, NRS e-invoicing readiness, and state levies), and force completely different industries into rigid, one-size-fits-all screens.

## 2. Core User Flows (How Someone Uses It) *(Conductor Tracks Initialized in Phase 2)*
1. **Step 1 (Dynamic Business Profile Onboarding):** A user picks one of the **6 Flagship Reference Packs** OR launches the **Universal Custom/Composable Pack Builder** (e.g., for a *Hotel/Hospitality*, *Diner/Restaurant*, *Clinic*, or a *Hybrid Multi-Unit* business combining multiple profiles) and sets their Nigerian entity/tax profile (legal form: Ltd / Business Name / Non-Profit Incorporated Trustee, state, turnover/asset bracket under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, TIN, VAT/WHT/State levy status). `NairaBooks` automatically configures the tailored Chart of Accounts, tax matrix, operational modules, UI labels, and KPI dashboard.
2. **Step 2 (Industry-Tailored Daily Operations -> Automatic Nigerian IFRS Ledger):** Teams work inside workflows matched to their business—POS & e-commerce stock sync, multi-tier warehouse POs & landed costs, BOM assembly & WIP production, job-site costing & subcontractor WHT, time/retainer billing & expense claims, or fund/donor budgets (as well as composable table/POS or room/folio workflows)—while the double-entry engine posts balanced NGN/FX IFRS journals automatically.
3. **Step 3 (Nigerian Statutory, Fund & IFRS Reporting):** Owners, accountants, and trustees generate **IFRS / IFRS for SMEs** statements (or **IPSAS/IFRS NGO Fund & Budget-vs-Actual** reports) plus Nigerian **Other National Disclosures** (*Statement of Value Added*, *Five-Year Financial Summary*), **CAMA 2020 Section 405** certifications, NRS-ready e-invoice tax structures, and monthly VAT/WHT/State tax schedules.

## 4. Explicit Scope Boundaries (What We Are NOT Building Yet)
- **Sequencing Strategy (`ADR-004`):** Build the **Universal Composable Industry Engine** alongside the **6 Flagship Reference Packs** (*Retail & E-commerce*, *Wholesale & Distribution*, *Manufacturing Light/Small Scale*, *Construction & Trades*, *Professional Services*, *Non-Profit Organizations*), then expose the **Universal Custom / Composable Pack Builder** (enabling *Hotels/Hospitality*, *Diners/Restaurants*, *Healthcare*, *Logistics*, and *Hybrid Multi-Unit* setups from the exact same primitives).
- **Wizard Boundaries (`Q7_AI`, `Q7_AUTH`, `Q11`):** In-app AI chat/OCR scanning, SaaS card checkout/billing, and 24/7 enterprise crash observability dashboards remain out of v1 scope unless re-enabled in the wizard.
- **Statutory Safety Rule:** All Nigerian tax rates, exemption thresholds, and state levies remain in effective-dated, inspectable rule tables (`Phase 3/4`)—never hardcoded in UI code.
