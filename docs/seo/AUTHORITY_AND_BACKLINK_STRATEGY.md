- **Partnership Model:** Co-authored continuing professional development (CPD) webinars on *"Modern Cloud Accounting & CAMA 2020 Section 405 Certification."*
- **Backlink Asset:** Permanent listing in ICAN's tech tools and software partner directory (`icanig.org`).

### 2. Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (CITN)
- **Partnership Model:** Collaborative whitepaper on *"Navigating Withholding Tax Credit Offsets Under the 2024/2025 FIRS Gazette."*
- **Backlink Asset:** Reference citations on CITN research and publication portals.

### 3. Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency of Nigeria (SMEDAN)
- **Partnership Model:** Providing free starter accounting toolkits for registered SMEDAN business clubs across the 36 states.
